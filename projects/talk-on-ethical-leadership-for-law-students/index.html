TALK ON ETHICAL LEADERSHIP FOR LAW STUDENTS

June 27, 2026

Rotary Club of Naga Heights President Atty. Khristian Damielle Jamer served as the invited resource speaker during the Association of Law Students of the Philippines (ALSP) National Convention, where he delivered a lecture on Ethical Student Leadership for Law Students. The session emphasized integrity, accountability, ethical decision-making, and servant leadership, encouraging future members of the legal profession to cultivate character and professional responsibility from their years in law school. Attended by law student delegates from different law schools across the Philippines, the lecture promoted values-based leadership and reinforced the role of lawyers as servants of justice and nation-building. Through the participation of its Club President, the Rotary Club of Naga Heights advanced Rotary’s commitment to youth development, vocational service, and the formation of ethical leaders who will create lasting impact in their communities and the legal profession.
